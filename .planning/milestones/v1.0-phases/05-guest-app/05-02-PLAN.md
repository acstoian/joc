Deliver the core gameplay vertical slice: the guest sees a live question, taps A or B to lock their answer (immediate, irreversible, refresh-proof), and sees the correct/wrong reveal in-place when the host reveals.

Purpose: This is the heart of the guest experience — the A/B tap with optimistic locking and the in-place reveal feedback.
